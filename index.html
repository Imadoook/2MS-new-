2MS Competency-Based Approach (CBA) Profile

In alignment with the National Curriculum Council didactic guidelines, the second year of middle school (2MS) consolidates foundational competencies from MS1 while deepening communicative fluency, reading comprehension (55 words target), and structured descriptive writing (35 words target)[cite: 123, 126, 127].

Global Competence

Understand, interact orally, and produce short oral and simple written messages of predominantly descriptive and narrative types[cite: 123].

National Identity

Be proud of one's cultural heritage, national values, and local community traditions[cite: 120, 123].

Citizenship

Show respect for others' feelings and tolerate differences in preferences, habits, and daily routines[cite: 123].

Openness to World

Demonstrate genuine curiosity to learn about different lifestyles, international cultures, and global habits[cite: 123].

Sequence 1: Habits & Preferences

Pedagogical progression for Middle School Year 2 following the standard national sequence layout[cite: 128, 130].

S1

Get Ready (Initial Situation)[cite: 130, 131]

1 Hour[cite: 130]

Presents the targeted problem-solving situation. Diagnostic assessment to collect initial representations stored in student portfolio[cite: 131].

S2

Listen & Interact[cite: 130, 132]

1 Hour[cite: 130]

Oral comprehension & interaction based on authentic listening scripts (Jack & Emma). Focus on predicting, gist, and specific scanning[cite: 95, 132, 133].

S3

Listen & Consider[cite: 130, 135]

1 Hour[cite: 130]

Language forms discovery from listening input. Focus on Adverbs of Frequency (always, sometimes, never) and Present Simple forms[cite: 95, 125, 135].

S4

Read & Interpret[cite: 130, 137]

1 Hour[cite: 130]

Written comprehension (~55 words text). Decoding, repeated reading for fluency, skimming, scanning layout, and key vocabulary practice[cite: 126, 137, 138, 139].

S5

Read & Consider[cite: 130, 141]

1 Hour[cite: 130]

Contextualized grammar discovery from reading. Focus on preference verbs (prefer, like, enjoy + -ing/noun) and Wh-questions[cite: 95, 125, 141, 158].

S6

Write Together (Guided Writing)[cite: 130, 143]

1 Hour[cite: 130]

Collaborative process writing. Brainstorming, mind-mapping, outlining, drafting, and revising a paragraph on habits with peers[cite: 143, 145, 146].

S7

Write Alone (Individual Writing)[cite: 130, 148]

1 Hour[cite: 130]

Autonomous production (~35 words) describing personal daily routine and preferences. Evaluation using criteria checklist[cite: 127, 148, 150].

S8

Check Your Progress (Evaluation & Portfolio)[cite: 130, 151, 155]

1 Hour[cite: 130]

Summative check using initial Get Ready task. Integration into 'My Portfolio' project followed by remediation pause[cite: 129, 151, 155].

2MS Sequence 1 Listening Scripts

Listen & Interact Script[cite: 95] Yard Conversation[cite: 95]

Emma: Jack! What do you do every day?[cite: 95]

Jack: Well, I always wake up at 7 o’clock. First, I get ready for school and then I have my breakfast.[cite: 95]

Emma: Me too! But I sometimes wake up late. After that, I eat breakfast. I always eat toast. I don’t like milk. I prefer drinking juice. What about you?[cite: 95]

Jack: I always have cereals with milk. After that, I pack my bag and ride my bike to school.[cite: 95]

Emma: I never go to school by bus, I enjoy walking. It’s fun![cite: 95]

Jack: Nice! Maybe I will walk sometimes, too![cite: 95]

Check Your Progress Script[cite: 96] Daily Routine Exchange[cite: 96]

Peter: Hi Sarah, how is your day going?[cite: 96]

Sarah: Oh, hello Peter. My day always starts at 6:30. But I don’t get out of bed immediately. First, I get ready and have a hot chocolate for my breakfast. Then, I go to school on foot.[cite: 96]

Peter: I prefer eating a fruit during the break at school.[cite: 96]

Sarah: Nice! What do you do in the evening?[cite: 96]

Peter: Well, I enjoy playing video games. Ah, I sometimes play basketball too. After that, I revise my lessons and go to bed.[cite: 96]

Sarah: I rarely exercise; I’m not a sporty person. I prefer reading stories.[cite: 96]

Peter: Well, we have different habits and preferences![cite: 96]

Sample Lesson Framework: Listen & Consider (2MS)[cite: 135]

Target: Adverbs of Frequency & Routine Verbs[cite: 125, 135]
Framework Stage: Discovery - Practice - Use[cite: 135, 136]
Perceptual Style: VAKT (Visual, Auditory, Kinesthetic)[cite: 105]
Interaction: T-S, S-S Pairwork[cite: 136]

Stage 1: Presentation & Context Discovery[cite: 135]

Re-isolate targeted chunks from Jack and Emma's dialogue[cite: 95, 135]: "I always wake up at 7 o'clock", "I sometimes wake up late", "I never go to school by bus."[cite: 95] Elicit student awareness using CCQs (Concept Checking Questions) on frequency position[cite: 141].

Stage 2: Mechanical & Guided Practice[cite: 135, 136]

Repetition drills, substitution drills using activity cards (e.g., [always/eat breakfast], [never/drink soda])[cite: 135, 136]. Substitution pair work: "What do you do in the morning? I always..."[cite: 95, 136]

Stage 3: Communicative Use & Personalization[cite: 136]

Students interview their peer regarding their daily habits and preferences, recording responses into a survey table[cite: 136].
